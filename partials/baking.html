Baking

I like figuring out the science of baking as much as the baking itself — why a dough behaves the way it does, what happens if you change one variable, which steps actually matter. These are the recipes I have landed on, written the way I would want them written: weights, not cups, and a note on what to look for rather than just how long to wait.

Each one opens as its own page, so you can bookmark it, send someone the link, or hit Print to get a clean one-page version for the kitchen.
